# Privacy Policy

Facets is a free, open-source viewer for STL, 3MF and OBJ files on iPhone, iPad and Mac, published by Tallack Media Corp.

**Facets collects no data.** It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network connections of its own.

- **Your models stay yours.** Files you open or save are read on your device. Facets doesn't upload, share or inspect them anywhere else.
- **iCloud Drive.** If iCloud Drive is turned on for Facets, your library is a Facets folder in your own iCloud Drive, synced by Apple between your devices. Facets has no access to it beyond what's on your device, and neither does Tallack Media Corp. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Folders you add.** Facets can only see folders and files you choose in the system picker, and keeps that permission on your device.
- **Settings and recents.** Your preferences, recent files and thumbnails are stored on your device (thumbnails for your library may also be kept in its iCloud Drive folder so your other devices can show them).
- **Links.** The About screen links to this project on GitHub, which opens in your browser.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request, correct or delete. You can check all of this yourself: the source code is at [github.com/Tallack-Media-Corp/facets](https://github.com/Tallack-Media-Corp/facets).

Questions: open an issue at [github.com/Tallack-Media-Corp/facets/issues](https://github.com/Tallack-Media-Corp/facets/issues).
